The Story

From Enterprise IT to
AI Pioneer

Tony's career began in the trenches of enterprise IT — engineering networks, managing complex systems, and solving real problems for real organizations. From the Walt Disney Internet Group to national automotive organizations, to building his first company tekGIANTS, Inc. from the ground up, he's always been driven by one thing: using technology to create measurable impact.

After years leading cloud infrastructure and solutions architecture at firms like Vertek Corporation and Rudram Engineering, Tony saw the AI revolution coming long before most. He founded Rovio AI to bridge the gap between cutting-edge artificial intelligence and the businesses that need it most — but don't have the time or expertise to navigate it alone.

Today, Tony works directly with founders, executives, and operations teams to identify AI opportunities, design automation systems, and coach organizations through lasting digital transformation.
